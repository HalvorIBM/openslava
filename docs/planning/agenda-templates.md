Use these as starting points. Adjust timing based on selected labs, client pace, and available CE staffing.

**Best for:** Executive preview, value framing, first exposure to Bob.

| Time | Activity | Duration | Who |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 – 0:10 | Welcome & Objectives | 10 min | CE Lead |
| 0:10 – 0:25 | Bob Fundamentals — what Bob is, example use cases | 15 min | CE Lead |
| 0:25 – 0:45 | Live Mini Demo — guided walkthrough of a key use case | 20 min | CE Lead |
| 0:45 – 1:15 | Quick Guided Mini-Lab — participants create a basic workflow | 30 min | All attendees |
| 1:15 – 1:30 | Wrap-up & Next Steps | 15 min | CE Lead |

## Template 5: Innovation Jam Format *(Competitive / Multi-Team)*

**Best for:** Strategic enterprise accounts, multiple teams competing for pilot priority, executive visibility, and multi-day or full-day hackathons.

The Innovation Jam extends standard Bobathon hands-on labs with structured problem framing, team coaching, executive judges, rubric-based scoring, and a leadership readout.

### Additional Staffing & Roles

* **Executive Judges:** Score team presentations against a business value rubric, provide directional feedback, and select winning use cases.
* **Team Coaches:** CE technical and domain coaches embedded with teams to refine use case problem statements, guide storytelling, and run pre-readout playback sessions.
* **Note Takers:** Record all MVP candidates, open action items, and executive feedback during presentations.

### Innovation Jam Schedule Overview

| Time | Block | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| **Morning** | Problem Framing & Workspace Setup | Teams define target use case, As-Is assessment, opportunity statement, and initialize Bob workspace |
| **Midday** | Guided Build & Team Hacking | Hands-on prototyping using Bob modes, workflows, and MCP integrations with CE coach support |
| **Afternoon** | Playback & Pitch Prep | Teams refine business value narrative, prepare slides using readout templates, and run a dry-run playback with coaches |
| **Final Hour** | Executive Readout & Judging | 5–7 min presentations per team, Q&A with Executive Judges, rubric scoring, winner announcement, and MVP next steps |

### Closing Sequence & Executive Deliverables

1. **Pre-readout Playback:** Coaches validate presentation flows, ensuring technical feasibility matches business value claims.
2. **Executive Readout:** Facilitated pitches scored across Innovation, Feasibility, Business Impact, and Bob Adoption.
3. **MVP Identification:** Note takers record top MVP candidates with named sponsors and target pilot dates.
4. **Executive Summary Package (within 5 business days):** Leadership recap deck, opportunity matrix, prioritized modernization roadmap, and pilot engagement proposal.

## Customizing Your Agenda

When building a custom agenda, use the [Bobathon Builder Mode](build-with-bob.md) to generate and adjust the schedule. Key principles:

- **Sequential labs:** Everyone does the same thing at the same time — best for first events and smaller groups
- **Parallel labs:** Teams split by use case into breakout rooms — better for larger groups and multiple tracks
- **Buffer time:** Client attendees take 2–5x longer than IBM practitioners — always add 20–30% buffer
- **The baking show:** Every lab should have a final endpoint snapshot so CE can "skip ahead" if time runs short
- **Debrief matters:** Reserve at least 20–25 min at the end — this is where the pilot proposal conversation starts
