# How to Engage Client Engineering

Bobathons are delivered through the IBM Client Engineering engagement model. There is **no cost to the client** for CE engagements.

---

## Step 1: Identify the Opportunity

You need an active opportunity record in IBM Sales Cloud (ISC) before submitting. Confirm:

- [ ] Opportunity exists in ISC
- [ ] Account team is aligned
- [ ] Client contact and scope are identified

---

## Step 2: Submit a Deal Support Request (DSR)

From the Opportunity record in ISC:

1. Navigate to the **Related List** → **Quick Links** → **Deal Support Requests**
2. Click **New** (upper right)
3. Fill in the **"How can we help"** field:
    ```
    Bob-a-thon for [Client Name]
    
    Request: Plan and deliver a Bobathon hands-on workshop
    Skills needed: IBM Bob, [Java / IBM i / IBM Z / SDLC — choose relevant]
    Business challenge: [Brief description of client pain point]
    Estimated attendees: [N developers]
    Preferred timing: [Rough date range]
    ```
4. Set **Opportunity Product** to the relevant product
5. Set **When is the support needed**
6. **Submit**

A Client Engineer will be assigned and contact you within 1–2 business days.

---

## Timeline Expectations

| Scenario | Minimum Lead Time |
|---|---|
| Standard Bobathon using provided sample apps (GenApp, Flight400, etc.) | **1 week** |
| Bobathon using client's own code or custom use cases | **3+ weeks** |
| Bobathon requiring TechZone environment provisioning | **1 week** (request immediately after scoping) |
| Bobathon requiring Bob AI Security Review at client | **Allow 2–4 weeks extra** |

!!! warning "Don't commit to a date before the CE is assigned"
    Lead times for environment provisioning (especially pp4z for Z) are fixed. Committing a date before confirming CE availability and environment readiness is the most common cause of scrambled events.

---

## CE Staffing Guidelines

| Attendee Count | CE Support Recommended |
|---|---|
| 4–10 developers | 1 CE Lead |
| 10–15 developers | 1 CE Lead + 1 CE support |
| 15+ developers | 1 CE Lead + additional CEs (1 per ~10 attendees) |
| Parallel use case tracks | 1 CE per breakout room |
