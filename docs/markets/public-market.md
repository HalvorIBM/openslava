# Public Market

!!! note "Market-Specific Reference Only"
    The governance policies, loaner hardware fleet, contact points, and internal tracking links on this page apply specifically to the **US Public Market**. Practitioners in other markets or geographies should refer to their respective regional leadership and enablement channels.

## 🏛️ Public Market Format & Travel Governance

* **2026 Default Format:** For the remainder of 2026, the default standard for Bobathons in the Public Market is **Virtual**.
* **In-Person Events:** Reserved for specific strategic engagements and requires approval from First-Line Managers (FLM) and Market Director.
* **Travel Submissions:** In-person travel submissions must be coordinated with leadership overseeing Public Market travel (Frank Attai's office) before committing dates to the client.

---

## 💻 Loaner Laptop Fleet (CE Lab Fleet)

The Public Market CE team maintains a shared fleet of 20 pre-configured loaner laptops for in-person events where client machines cannot be used.

!!! warning "Approval Required Before Committing Client Dates"
    Loaner laptops are shared across all CE engagements. Requests must be coordinated with **Connie Wu** via the booking note before committing dates to clients. Shipping charges above **$1,000** require Public Market CE Leader approval; above that threshold, Public Market CFO approval is required. Shipping is charged to the CE FLM credit card.

### Fleet Specifications

| Spec | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Hardware** | 20 × Lenovo ThinkPad (Windows 10) |
| **Chargers** | 20 chargers (*Note: 1 charger is under-powered — keep labeled and in reserve*) |
| **Cases & Security** | 4 Pelican cases with 2 padlocks each. **Lock code for all locks: `1911`** |
| **Power Warning** | Keep laptops plugged in at all times — batteries discharge quickly |
| **Booking Box Note** | [`ibm.ent.box.com/notes/2272777194902`](https://ibm.ent.box.com/notes/2272777194902) (Provide name, contact info, location, and dates of use) |

### Pre-Installed Fleet Software

* **Runtimes & Languages:** Python 3.12, Node.js, Java 8, Java 21 (IBM Semeru), Maven, `uv`
* **Tools & Extensions:** Bob (IDE), Bob CLI, bob4z (VS Code extension for Z), ACE Toolkit, Eclipse, Git, WSL, Podman + Podman Compose
